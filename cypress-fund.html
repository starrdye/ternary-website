Investment Strategy

Ternary Cypress Fund deploys a value investing strategy, trading public equities in which the underlying asset value exceeds the market value of the stock in undervalued industries. Our approach focuses on identifying companies with strong fundamentals, solid management teams, and sustainable competitive advantages that are currently trading below their intrinsic value.

We conduct thorough fundamental analysis, including financial statement analysis, industry research, and competitive positioning to identify these undervalued opportunities. Our investment process is disciplined and research-driven, ensuring that we only invest in companies that meet our strict valuation criteria.
